# Dataset strategy and proposed joins — M0

These are design decisions, not executed joins. Do not concatenate unrelated samples or equate distinct hazard targets.

## Separate experiments

| Experiment | Proposed row unit / target | Scope and first feasibility gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Biological | Source sample × selected pathogen assay × prediction cutoff; `pathogen_positive` | FSIS commodity/program pilot. Preserve explicit negatives, untested/indeterminate outcomes and assay methods. Group multiple tests from the same sample, lot and establishment as appropriate. Estimate positivity among sampled products, not population prevalence. |
| Chemical | PDP sample × analyte; `chemical_exceedance` | Need applicable commodity/analyte/jurisdiction/effective-date limit plus units, LOD/LOQ and residue definition. Detection alone is not exceedance. Untested analytes are not negative. |
| Spoilage/quality | Experimental batch/run × sampling time; microbial count or validated spoilage class | Milk, paneer and beef remain separate experiments initially. Verify CFU/g vs CFU/ml vs log10 units, replicate/run identities, assay timing and how class labels were derived. |
| Cold-storage condition proxy | Fruit environmental observation; `storage_condition_class` | Good/Bad may encode an environmental rule. This can test condition classification, not demonstrate measured pathogen or spoilage prediction. |
| Operational demo | Synthetic batch graph/sensor events | Plumbing and decision demonstrations only, with `is_synthetic=true`; no real predictive-accuracy or biological calibration claims. |

M1 primary acquisition: FSIS raw poultry sampling data and documentation; local openFDA registration as context. Small sensor metadata/file inspection may establish the next experiment, but do not ingest all eleven sources indiscriminately.

## Proposed join matrix

For every enrichment retain match method, source version, event time, availability time, matched/unmatched counts and ambiguity counts. Aggregate on the right before joining when needed; assert one output row per original observation. Never silently multiply rows.

| Proposed relationship | Entity, granularity and keys | Temporal/geographic/product validity | Decision / leakage and cardinality controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FSIS sample → FSIS establishment metadata | Source establishment ID, including prefixes/identifier namespaces; sample-level left to effective establishment version | Same FSIS program; metadata effective and known at cutoff | Conditional. Current directory attributes may contain future changes. Use historical version or exclude time-varying attributes. Many samples to one effective establishment row; sample/assay count must remain unchanged. |
| FSIS sample → FDA inspection | FSIS establishment ID and FDA FEI are different systems | A verified physical-facility crosswalk with effective dates is required; product jurisdiction may differ | **Blocked by missing crosswalk.** Name/product similarity is insufficient. Only finalized classifications available before prediction; aggregate unique inspection events before left join. |
| Sample → previous tests at same facility | Namespaced facility ID + organism + commodity/program/assay definition | Prior specimen event time `< T` and result availability `< T` | Conditional. Exclude focal sample, its duplicate assays and any unresolved same-lot/group dependence. Denominator contains comparable finalized tests only. No history → missing rate with zero observed count, not a zero risk claim. |
| Supplier/facility → recall history | Validated legal-entity/facility crosswalk + historical recall events | Firm address is not farm origin. Use only event information released `< T`; event_id groups related product recalls | Conditional; currently blocked for supplier-level joining. Count events and product recall records separately. Later termination/status updates must not travel backward in time. A firm-level match cannot be represented as farm evidence. |
| Batch/sample → NOAA weather | Actual location → documented station mapping (distance/elevation/coverage), then completed time intervals | A known farm/processing location and timezone are required; seven-day windows end before cutoff and only use available observations | Conditional. Retail/recalling-firm address cannot stand in for farm. Same-day daily maxima/rain totals are unavailable intraday. Missing station coverage stays missing; never nearest-date future matching. |
| Sample → RASFF contextual history | Origin country + harmonized commodity/hazard + historical notifications | Country of origin must actually be known; notification availability `< T`; mapping and export version retained | Conditional, aggregate once per country/commodity/cutoff. Counts are surveillance signals with no sampled-food denominator. Export revisions/follow-ups require availability controls. No sample-level linkage or probability from notification frequency. |
| Malaysian observation → OpenDOSM agricultural context | Actual state/district + crop species + year/version | Malaysian crops only; annual production values may be released years later; country/state/national total rows must be excluded from district join | Conditional, currently not applicable to US FSIS records. Use a release published before T, never January 1 of the reference year as availability. No direct contamination label or sample-level production inference. |
| Milk tank sensors → milk lab results | Same farm/tank and collection/cleaning cycle or documented lot; aggregate pre-sample sensor window | One farm does not imply every reading describes every lab sample; result availability is a delayed target | Conditional; publisher file and cycle linkage need verification. Many sensor points → one sample feature vector. Do not nearest-match future lab samples or forward-fill labels across cleaning/refill cycles. |
| Paneer environmental log → paneer assays | Verified experiment/run/treatment/replicate and elapsed sampling time | Same experiment and exposure window, not merely same food or spreadsheet date | Conditional; file-level gate unresolved. Repeated runs and destructive specimens require explicit unit mapping. Do not duplicate a sparse assay over thousands of sensor rows. |
| PDP samples → residues/lookups/limits | Publisher's documented composite sample keys + analyte; versioned commodity and pesticide codes | Within the same survey/schema year; limits use jurisdiction, commodity, residue definition, valid dates and units | Conditional. One sample has many analyte results. Remain at sample×analyte grain or define aggregation explicitly. Do not multiply with many lookup/limit versions; test uniqueness and detection qualifiers. |
| Beef or fruit → FSIS or PDP by product name | Shared commodity label only, no specimen/facility/lot linkage | Different experiments, places, times, assays and sampling selection | **Rejected.** Same product does not identify the same sample. No cross-source imputation of pH, sensors, label or outcome. |
| Recalls/inspection classes → biological labels | Enforcement/classification record versus lab specimen | Recall reason can include allergens, labeling, foreign bodies or suspected contamination | **Rejected as ground truth contamination mapping.** History may enrich only under the controls above. Unlisted entities/products are not clean negatives. |

## Two clocks and split policy

At prediction time T, predictor events must satisfy `event_time < T` **and** `available_at < T` for strict historical counts. Contemporaneous sensors may use `observed_at <= T` and `available_at <= T` if the operational cutoff includes them. Date-only records use conservative full-day boundaries; do not order an unknown-time result ahead of a morning prediction.

Never use observed final shipment duration, final maximum temperature or lab result as a predictor for a decision made before those values existed. Derive partial-duration and prefix-window features as of T. Horizon and target sample time must be fixed before splitting.

Person B should receive temporal train/validation/test partitions (nominally earliest 70% / next 15% / final 15%) at **unique time boundaries**, preserving whole sample/lot/experimental groups. Percentages are approximate after grouping. Evaluate facility-held-out performance separately to measure new-facility generalization; temporal and facility splits answer different deployment questions. A single-farm/run dataset cannot validate cross-farm performance.

Assign splits before fitting imputation, category encoders, feature selection or scaling. Training examples and validation tuning must respect delayed label availability; exclude/purge training labels unavailable at the training cutoff. Overlapping windows/shared batches may require a gap and group purge. Historical features on test rows may use newly available past outcomes only if the declared evaluation simulates that online updating policy; otherwise freeze history at training end. Choose and document one policy rather than silently using all test labels.
