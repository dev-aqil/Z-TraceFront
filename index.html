Build an AI solution with meaningful use of LinuxONE Community Cloud.

Project checklist

  • Choose one challenge track and a measurable community problem.
  • Use documented public or authorised data.
  • Run the AI or inference component on LinuxONE.
  • Prepare a working demo and a maximum three-minute video.

Minimum architecture

Data enters the system, the model runs on LinuxONE, and the resulting decision or alert is shown through a simple interface.
