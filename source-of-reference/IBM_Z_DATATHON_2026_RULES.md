# IBM Z Datathon 2026: Working Rules Extract

> Source: [Official Rules and Prize Guidelines](https://www.starhacks.org/ibmzdatathon-official-rule)  
> Extracted: 27 September 2026  
> This is a project-ready summary, not a replacement for the official rules.

## Non-negotiable project constraints

- Team: *4 to 6* currently enrolled college or university students. Each person may join one team only.
- Registration closes: *1 October 2026*.
- Main event: *17 to 18 October 2026*. Build the project during the official hacking period only.
- Submit one project per challenge track through Hack2Skill before its submission deadline.
- The project code and idea must be original to the participating team and created during the event. Open-source libraries and frameworks are allowed, but link them in the submission.
- The project must use AI and meaningfully integrate IBM Z through the *LinuxONE Community Cloud* platform. Include evidence of platform use, such as screenshots of the instance, model, security, containers, scalability, or compute capabilities.
- Complete the two required preparatory labs before building:
  1. Predictive AI Hybrid AI scenarios (multi-model)
  2. Predictive AI plus Generative AI

## Dataset position

The rules do *not* prescribe a mandatory dataset or a data download. Teams must provide supporting data that validates the problem and demonstrate a sound data methodology.

Use a legitimate, documented public or properly authorised dataset. Do not use personal, confidential, or restricted data without a clear right to use it. Record its source, licence, limitations, and any privacy treatment in the README and pitch.

## Choose one track

| Track | What the solution should demonstrate |
| --- | --- |
| Real-Time AI for Critical Decisions | Streaming or transactional data, risk or anomaly detection, prediction, alerts, and decision support where timing matters. |
| AI Secured: Innovation Without Exposure | AI value with security, privacy, identity, encryption, access control, trust, compliance, or quantum-safe readiness designed in. |
| Wildcard: Beyond the Obvious | An original AI plus data solution outside the other tracks, with a clear problem, IBM Z integration, responsible AI, technical ambition, and measurable impact. |

## Required final submission

1. A *maximum 3-minute demo video* covering:
   - the problem;
   - supporting data validating it;
   - solution overview;
   - IBM Z contribution, technology stack, and a demo of the LinuxONE instance and model.
2. A complete GitHub or GitLab repository, public or accessible to reviewers. A ZIP is accepted as an alternative.
3. Links to every open-source library or framework used.
4. Project title formatted as `[Team Name_IBM Z Datathon 2026 Project]`.
5. Optional but recommended: architecture flowchart and a working prototype.

## How judging works

The standard judging score is *40 points*, four equal 10-point categories:

| Category | What earns a strong score |
| --- | --- |
| Community value and theme alignment | A meaningful real-world problem, clear beneficiaries, and direct AI-for-Good alignment with the selected track. |
| Novelty and originality | A distinct idea and code built from scratch during the Datathon. |
| Technical design and LinuxONE use | Clear architecture, AI/ML running on LinuxONE Community Cloud, appropriate open-source AI/ML packages, and visible use of platform capabilities. |
| Execution and delivery | A complete, clear, professional pitch and working demo within the video limit. |

The regional AI Innovation Excellence Awards place extra emphasis on originality plus transformational impact and technical excellence on LinuxONE.

## Prizes and regional context

- Malaysia is an APAC in-person hub.
- Each listed regional and in-person competition category awards: first *USD 1,000*, second *USD 750*, third *USD 500*.
- Winning teams receive six months of extended LinuxONE Community Cloud access, stated as a total value of USD 20,000+.
- IBM Z Day showcase: *18 November 2026*.

## Legal and eligibility notes worth keeping in mind

- Eligible participants are 18+ university students, registered on Hack2Skill before the deadline.
- Employees, contractors, interns, officers, and immediate family of IBM or the organiser are ineligible.
- The contest excludes jurisdictions where participation is prohibited. The rules explicitly list Brazil, Italy, Quebec, China, and US-embargoed or OFAC-designated countries.
- Participants retain project IP, subject to sponsor content and third-party rights. Submissions are not confidential and may be promoted by IBM or Shooting Stars Foundation.
- The organisers may collect registration personal data to administer the event and prizes.
