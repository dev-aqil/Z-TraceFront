## Before the event

- Every person registers individually, then the team lead creates the 4 to 6 person team in Hack2Skill.
- Submit an abstract by 5 October. The guidebook defines it as a 300 to 500 word summary of the problem, proposed approach, dataset(s), and why IBM Z is the right platform.
- Attend the mandatory LinuxONE readiness session. It covers credentials, connectivity, LinuxONE access, and the submission platform. Problems discovered after the event begins may not qualify for an extension.
- Public or legally licensed datasets, APIs, and standard libraries are permitted. There is no named mandatory dataset.

## What must be delivered

- Maximum three-minute video: problem, supporting data, solution, technology stack, LinuxONE instance, and model demonstration.
- GitHub/GitLab repository link or ZIP.
- Correct title: `[Team Name_IBM Z Datathon 2026 Project]`.
- Links for open-source resources used.
- Recommended, not mandatory: design flowchart and working prototype.

## Competition and technical rules

- Start the actual project code during the 17 to 18 October hacking window. Pre-event research, data exploration, abstraction, and architecture planning are allowed.
- Do not reuse a previous personal project or commercial codebase as the core submission.
- Open-source libraries, public APIs, and IBM-provided tools are allowed when appropriately attributed and licensed.
- Use LinuxONE meaningfully. The platform provides the enterprise compute environment; your team supplies the laptop, connection, and development tools.
- Two AI/ML labs are mandatory prerequisites: Predictive AI Hybrid AI scenarios (multi-model), and Predictive AI plus Generative AI.

## How to maximise the 40-point score

| Category | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Community value and theme | Define a real problem, beneficiary, and measurable positive outcome. |
| Novelty | Show why the approach is genuinely different, not just a generic dashboard. |
| Technical design | Run meaningful AI/ML work on LinuxONE and show security, containers, scaling, or compute evidence. |
| Execution | Make the three-minute story clear, polished, and demo-led. |

## Useful project boundaries

- A working LinuxONE-hosted inference API plus a simple dashboard is enough for a strong demo.
- Collect screenshots of the VM, deployment, model request, response, and any security control while building.
- Keep the architecture simple enough to prove live. A speculative multi-service system scores less than a completed, clearly demonstrated solution.

Use the official Discord server for announcements, team formation, general help, and IBM Z help during the event. Use the organiser's issue form for unresolved problems. Do not publish event credentials, Jupyter tokens, SSH private keys, or access URLs in this repository.
